Customer authorisation

You expressly authorise Click2Check to conduct a search for, and if available obtain, details of your employment and income information, including your salary, job title, length of service and place of work (“Employment Data”). This will be used to assess .

A search for your Employment and Income Data will be conducted via our partner Equifax Limited, in accordance with its privacy policy, and a request will be sent to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC). You will be directed to a web address asking you to provide your HMRC authentication details and enable disclosure of your Employment and Income Data. Click2Check will not have sight of or access to any of your HMRC authentication information.

You always have the option not to give your authorisation. Without it, Click2Check may not be able to provide certain products and services to you, or alternative verification of your Employment Data may be required, which may cause some delay.
